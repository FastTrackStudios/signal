Fretted instruments detune an articulation's voices from a per-position table, keep an articulation to some strings and below a fret, let a source leave its own release recording on a frozen counter, play side keys (fret noise, chokes, key FX) as one-shots with their own counters, slide in from the open string, and play free articulations once per doubled take; a key that places nothing no longer restarts the idle hand reset. Shreddage Argent's palm mute, power chords, fret noise, wide chokes, FX mode and slide-ins, and Darkwall's palm mute, fret noise, key FX and slide-ins play KONTRA's voices exactly (signal)
